# WildKind

A nature app that turns wildlife spotting into a collection game. Snap a photo of an animal, identify it, and add it to your personal dex. Designed and built solo by Pauline Shay.

*In development. Not yet on the App Store.*

## What it does

- **Identify:** take or upload a photo, and WildKind names the animal.
- **Dex:** every species you find is collected as a card, with rarity and kingdom.
- **Map:** see sightings and species near you, with a walking guide to nearby spots.
- **Progress:** badges, quests, streaks, XP and a weekly featured creature.
- **Family safe:** teen and parent permission flows during sign-up.

## My role

End to end: product design, visual design and engineering.

- Designed the brand, design system, icons, badges and every screen.
- Built the app in React Native with a token-based design system.
- Designed the onboarding, including consent flows for younger users.

## Tech stack

- Expo / React Native, Expo Router, TypeScript
- Supabase (auth, database, edge functions)
- Mapbox
- AI vision for species identification

## What's in this repo

A showcase of the design and front end:

- `app`, `screens`: routes and screens
- `components`: UI components
- `design`: design tokens, theme and atoms
- `assets`: brand, badge and illustration assets

Left out on purpose: the backend, the identification pipeline, data services, the species catalog and app configuration. This copy won't run as a full app.
